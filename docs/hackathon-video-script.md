# Hackathon Video Script

**Duration:** ~60 seconds

---

Hey, I'm [Your Name]. I'm building Xenga — an escrow and reputation protocol for stablecoin payments.

There's a reason stablecoin payments haven't gone mainstream. Right now, paying with stablecoins is like wiring cash to a stranger — no chargebacks, no buyer protection, nothing. Merchants won't accept it, buyers won't risk it. And on the other side, AI agents are starting to buy and sell services autonomously — but they have the exact same problem. No trust, no recourse, no history.

Xenga solves this for both. When a payment comes in — whether from a shopper or an AI agent — USDC gets locked in escrow on Base. Seller delivers, funds release. If something goes wrong, there's dispute resolution built into the protocol. No one has to blindly trust the other side.

And every transaction builds an on-chain reputation score. Completion rates, dispute history, transaction volume — all public, all permissionless. High reputation means faster settlement. New counterparty means longer escrow. Trust adjusts automatically.

Traditional commerce. Agent commerce. Same protocol, same safety.

I'm a solo founder. This is Xenga — the trust layer stablecoin payments have been missing.
